Privacy Policy
Sahara: The Odyssey of the Sands
Effective date: October 21, 2026
1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the game Sahara: The Odyssey of the Sands (the "Game") handles information. The Game is a free browser-based arcade game with 30 mini-game levels set in the Sahara desert. We respect your privacy and designed the Game to collect as little information as possible.
2. Information We Collect
We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. Specifically:
• The Game does not require an account, login, or registration.
• The Game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.
• The Game does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs.
• The Game does not send gameplay data to any server operated by the developer.
3. Data Stored on Your Device
To remember how far you have progressed, the Game saves one small piece of data (the highest level you have unlocked) in your browser's local storage. This data:
• stays on your device and is never transmitted to the developer;
• contains no personal information;
• can be erased at any time by clearing your browser's site data or local storage.
If local storage is unavailable or disabled in your browser, the Game still works, but your progress will not be saved between sessions.
4. Hosting and Third Parties
The Game is hosted on a third-party platform. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and device information) in order to deliver the page and keep the service secure. The developer does not receive or control this information. Please refer to the hosting provider's own privacy policy for details on how it handles such data.
The Game does not share any information with other third parties and contains no links to advertising networks.
5. Children's Privacy
The Game is suitable for a general audience, including children. Because the Game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).
6. Security
Since the Game does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data held by the developer to secure. The only data involved is your own progress value, stored locally in your browser.
7. Your Rights
Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. You can reset your saved progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you have questions about your privacy rights, you may contact us using the details below.
8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the Game. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated whenever changes are made. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
